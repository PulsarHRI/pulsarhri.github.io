# Actuator setup

This page covers physical setup before USB or CAN communication.

## What you need

Usually supplied by PulsarHRI:

- one PULSE actuator;
- one power bus cable.

Usually supplied by the user:

- a secure mechanical support and suitable screws;
- a 48 V DC power supply;
- a computer for configuration or control.

## Setup

1. Review [Safety and prerequisites](safety.md).
2. Mount the actuator using the documented [mechanical interfaces](mechanical_interfaces.md).
3. Connect the power cable to the actuator and power supply.
4. Verify polarity and wiring before switching on the supply.
5. Apply power and check the [status LED](led.md).
6. Continue with [USB](connectivity/usb.md) or [CAN FD](connectivity/can.md).

For the shortest first-use workflow, continue to [Single actuator with Pulsar App](../quickstart/pulsar_app.md).
